Study Overview

How Does Technology Influence Learning?

This study investigates how different instructional approaches influence student engagement and learning performance.

You will participate in a short educational activity and answer questions about what you learned.

What will you do?

  1. Read a short educational lesson.
  2. Complete a knowledge quiz.
  3. Answer questions about your learning experience.

Participants may receive different versions of the learning activity. Please follow the instructions shown on your screen.
