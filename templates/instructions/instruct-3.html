Participant Instructions

Before You Begin

Please follow these instructions carefully to ensure accurate study results.

  1. Complete the study in a quiet environment.
  2. Read the learning material carefully.
  3. Answer the quiz questions independently.
  4. Provide honest responses to the questionnaire.
  5. Complete the activity without unnecessary interruptions.

Learning Quiz

After the lesson, you will answer a short quiz measuring your understanding of the material.

Experience Questionnaire

You will rate your engagement and perceived usefulness of the learning activity on a five-point scale.
