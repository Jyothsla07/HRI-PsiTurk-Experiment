We need your consent to proceed

You are invited to participate in a research study examining how different instructional methods affect learning and understanding of Operating Systems concepts.

Purpose of Research: This study investigates how robot-based video instruction and computer-based learning materials influence students' understanding, engagement, and learning experiences.

What you will do in this study: If you agree to participate, you will complete a demographic questionnaire, indicate your familiarity with Round Robin scheduling, and complete an audio/video check. You will then study Round Robin and Weighted Round Robin CPU scheduling using the assigned learning materials, complete two quizzes, and answer a brief survey about your learning experience.

Time required: The study is expected to take approximately 40–50 minutes, depending on how long you spend reviewing the learning materials.

Risks and discomfort: No significant risks beyond those ordinarily associated with computer-based learning activities are anticipated.

Benefits: There may be no direct personal benefit from participating. The findings may help researchers better understand the effectiveness of different educational methods.

Confidentiality: Your responses will be collected for research purposes and handled in accordance with the approved study protocol. Research results may be reported in aggregate form without identifying individual participants.

Participation and Withdrawal: Participation is voluntary. You may choose not to participate or stop at any time without penalty.

Do you understand and consent to these terms?

By selecting "I Agree," you confirm that you have read and understood the information above and voluntarily agree to participate in this study.
